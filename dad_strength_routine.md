# Dad's Strength Routine (wrist-friendly)

Pushups hurt his wrists. He already does goblet squats (20 lb) and heel raises.

## Pushup alternatives (keep the wrist straight)

- **Pushups on dumbbells, pushup handles, or fists.** Wrist stays straight
  instead of bent back at 90°. Usually the quickest fix.
- **Incline pushups** on a counter or wall. Less weight goes through the wrists.
  Can be combined with handles.
- **Dumbbell floor press** or bench press. No weight on the hands at all.

## The most important ones to add

1. **Rows** (one-arm dumbbell row or band row): upper back, posture, balances
   out the pushing.
2. **Hip hinge** (dumbbell Romanian deadlift, or glute bridges to start):
   hamstrings, glutes, lower back. Trains picking things up off the floor.
3. **Farmer's carry**: walk holding heavy dumbbells. Grip, core, posture.
   Grip strength is one of the best predictors of healthy aging. Straight wrist, no bend.
4. **Step-ups or split squats**: single-leg strength and balance, which matter
   a lot for preventing falls.
5. **Core without the wrists**: forearm plank, dead bugs, bird dogs.
6. **Balance**: stand on one leg for 30 s per side, holding a counter if needed.

## Sample session (2–3x/week)

| Exercise                         | Sets x Reps       |
|----------------------------------|-------------------|
| Goblet squat                     | 3 x 8–12          |
| Pushups on handles/incline       | 3 x 8–12          |
| One-arm dumbbell row             | 3 x 10 each side  |
| Romanian deadlift / glute bridge | 3 x 10            |
| Farmer's carry                   | 3 x 30–40 steps   |
| Forearm plank or dead bug        | 3 x 20–30 s       |
| Heel raises                      | 2 x 15            |

Go up in weight when the last couple of reps feel easy. If his wrist hurts even
with a straight wrist or keeps hurting outside of workouts, see a doctor or
physical therapist.
